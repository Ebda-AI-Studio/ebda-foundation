# F7-HAMZA-2

Ruling F7-HAMZA-2 (controller 2026-11-23, supersedes F7-HAMZA-1) - Arabic search matches under TWO folds: a query term matches a document term when they agree under fold B OR under fold A. Fold B is the ORIGINAL Store fold (FDN-TEXT-1, ئ to ي, ؤ to و), byte for byte and with its SQL twin `ebda_ar_norm` untouched, so every equivalence it ever made still holds (`vectors/arabic.json`, the 35 original vectors, is the no-regression set; typed ي for ئ such as `سايل`, `رايع` keeps working). Fold A is the hamza fold: NFKC first, ء ئ ؤ all become و, a run of و collapses (`مسئول` = `مسؤول`, `سؤال` = `سوال` = `سئوال`, `سائل` = `ساءل`). One fold cannot do both without the wrong merge `مسيول` = `مسؤول`, so the match is deliberately not transitive. `سائل` and `سال` are NOT the same term under either fold (negative vector). Evidence: `vectors/arabic-hamza.json` (28 fold-A vectors, 5 equivalent groups, 12 same-term and 8 different-term pairs), `vectors/arabic-search.json`. Where it applies: `foldArabicHamza`, `searchWordPairs`, `indexTextBoth`, `sameSearchTerm`, hamza-aware `highlightSegments`; Store product search (PG: a second doc function, two new indexes built CONCURRENTLY and a second search function; DO: A-fold columns) and Chat's FTS (both forms in one text, per-word OR). Surfaces that keep fold B only (no regression, no gain): customer, order, SKU and admin quick search. Cost if wrong: the index stores a second form per product (about 2x the doc index) and a typed query costs one extra trigram arm; a hamza-seat variant that fold A maps to و can also match a plain و word (`رائع` finds `راوع`); Chat's single-text index lets an A-form word match inside the B-form half of a row, a rare false hit on a name or message.
